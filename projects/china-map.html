What it does

A timeline that redraws the whole map

Hold the timeline down for about 0.6 seconds and it zooms in around the current instant — keep holding and it drills all the way down to individual days, so you can stop on 9 February 1234 and watch the Jin dynasty's last emperor reign for half a day before Caizhou falls.

🎨
Territory blocks
Every coexisting polity gets a distinct colour with its dynasty name at the centroid; borders shift with real history (Han's Hexi corridor, Tang losing Longyou, the Song–Jin Huai River line, Qing ceding Outer Manchuria).
🧵
Contested stripes
Regions fought over, occupied, or of disputed allegiance render as two-colour diagonal stripes — Song–Jin Huainan, Mongol–Song Sichuan, Qing vs. the Taiping, WWII-occupied territory.
🪄
Automatic border refinement
Hand-drawn shapes are densified to 0.55°, neighbouring polities' shared edges are stitched seam-free, coastlines are clipped to real Natural Earth geography, then smoothed with two passes of Chaikin's algorithm.
👑
Reigning monarchs panel
A draggable side panel lists every monarch alive at the current instant — name, temple/posthumous name, era name and regnal year, birth/death, predecessor and successor, with a generated seal-style avatar.
📊
Polity detail cards
Click any territory for its capital, founder, downfall, population over time (historical census figures alongside modern scholarly estimates), a summary, and sourcing.
▶️
Playback & navigation
Auto-advance the timeline at three speeds; space bar to pause, arrow keys to nudge by a step, scroll to zoom the map, drag to pan.
On accuracy: Territory shapes are generalised from Tan Qixiang's Historical Atlas of China and similar sources — illustrative, not county-level scholarship. Xia–Shang–Zhou dates follow the Xia–Shang–Zhou Chronology Project; anything before 841 BCE is inferential. Ming/Qing and major turning points are dated to the day, Tang/Song/Liao/Jin/Yuan mostly to the month, earlier periods to the year. Full source list and precision notes are in the in-app "Sources" popup (20 references, 7 caveats).

🤝 Built with Claude, directed by me

I used Claude as an implementation assistant throughout: boilerplate generation, unit-test scaffolding, and accelerated front-end visuals. The engineering judgement is mine.

  • I conceptualised the architecture — the zero-dependency constraint, the keyframe-and-interpolate model for shifting borders, and the separation of historical data from the rendering engine.
  • I peer-reviewed the code and the historical data, and corrected the territory and dating claims against the atlases and chronologies cited in the Sources panel.
  • I debugged the inconsistencies and functional flaws — seams between neighbouring polities, coastline clipping against Natural Earth geography, timeline zoom behaviour, and centroid label placement.
  • I guided Claude throughout, deciding which periods and features to build next and how each should behave.
